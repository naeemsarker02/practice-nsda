Liberation War Archive

A digital collection preserving the documents, testimonies, and photographs of Bangladesh's 1971 Liberation War.

In 1971, the people of Bangladesh fought a nine-month war for independence that cost millions of lives and reshaped a nation. This archive exists to safeguard the primary sources of that struggle: the treaties and declarations that marked its turning points, the first-hand accounts of those who lived through it, and the photographs that recorded it. Our aim is to keep these records accessible, accurate, and presented with the respect this history deserves, for researchers, students, and the general public alike.
