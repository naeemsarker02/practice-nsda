Abdul Karim
NameAbdul Karim
Role / BackgroundGuerrilla Fighter, Mukti Bahini, Sector 8
RegionJessore
Year1971

Abdul Karim joined the Mukti Bahini as a young volunteer in 1971. In this recording he describes his training across the border, the ambush operations his unit carried out along the Jessore front, and his memories of the day Dhaka was liberated.

Rahima Begum
NameRahima Begum
Role / BackgroundCivilian Witness
RegionDhaka
Year1971

Rahima Begum was a schoolteacher in Dhaka during the war. She recalls the crackdown of 25 March 1971, sheltering neighbors in her home, and the difficult months that followed before the city was liberated in December.

NameDavid Whitfield
Role / BackgroundForeign Correspondent
RegionDhaka and Calcutta
Year1971

David Whitfield covered the war for an international news outlet, reporting from both Dhaka and the refugee camps around Calcutta. In this interview he discusses the challenges of reporting under wartime censorship and what he witnessed in the refugee camps.

RegionAgartala Border
Year1971

Dr. Farida Yasmin volunteered at a field hospital near the Agartala border, treating wounded freedom fighters and refugees. She speaks about the shortage of medical supplies, the resilience of her patients, and the lasting impact the war had on her career in medicine.

Major Ziaur Rahman
NameMajor Ziaur Rahman
Role / BackgroundSector Commander, Bengal Regiment
RegionChittagong
Year1971

This entry is a historical note rather than a personal interview, since no first-hand testimony is attributed to Major Ziaur Rahman here. On 27 March 1971, he broadcast a declaration of independence over Swadhin Bangla Betar Kendra in Kalurghat, on behalf of Sheikh Mujibur Rahman, calling on Bengalis to resist the army crackdown. He went on to command Sector 1 of the Mukti Bahini during the war and later served as a president of Bangladesh.
